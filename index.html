What I work on

Hybrid AI that combines formal reasoning with statistical learning, applied where knowledge changes fast and errors are costly.

Knowledge that stays correct over time

Ontologies, terminologies and knowledge graphs change with every release. I build methods that detect those changes and repair the mappings and annotations that depend on them.

Knowledge for health

Language, history and skills
